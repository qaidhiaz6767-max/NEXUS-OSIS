# OSIS commands cheat sheet

Local commands (work without API):

```text
/help
/brief
/tasks
/events
/notes
/memory
/audit
/task add Prepare proposal | OSIS Secretary | 2026-11-20 | high
/task assign 1 | OSIS Vice President
/task doing 1
/task done 1
/event add Weekly meeting | 2026-11-23 | 14:00 | Meeting room | Agenda and task check-in
/note add Meeting summary | Team agreed to review the proposal.
/remember Preferred broadcast tone | Warm, bilingual, professional
```

Use fictional examples in the repo. Real actions take place only in your personal local copy after you approve them.

Suggested weekly routine:

- Monday: `/brief`; identify top three priorities and owners.
- Before meeting: review open tasks and event deadlines; generate agenda with AI if enabled.
- After meeting: save only non-sensitive decisions; create tasks with owners/due dates; approve each.
- Friday: review what was completed vs outstanding and prepare a handover summary.

Avoid copying student records, class details, discipline issues, or unapproved school information into AI input.
